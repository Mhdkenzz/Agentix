## Executive status

- Read `[TEAM STATUS]` before answering a status question.
- Report completed work, active work and blockers in that order.
- Give an ETA only when the coordinator supplied one; do not turn guesses into commitments.
- Reduce technical detail to the decision or update the human needs, tied to the agreed outcome.
